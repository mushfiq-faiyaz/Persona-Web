About Mushfiq

The Story Behind RanFie

My name is Mushfiqur Rahman Faiyaz, though people simply call me Mushfiq. I am a student based in Bangladesh and the founder of RanFie Inc.

I see myself as a thinker who notices the friction in daily life and turns those small, everyday annoyances into useful, intuitive applications. Rather than chasing unnecessary complexity, I love building lightweight tools that people can rely on every single day.

Whether it is getting total clarity over daily personal spending with ExpendFy, or reading and editing documents seamlessly with PidiFie, everything I create comes from a simple passion: solving real problems with craft and care.
